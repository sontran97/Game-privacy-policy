2. Information we handle

Sign-in and social features (Game Center / Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games Services (Android), the Game may use your player alias and player ID to show leaderboards, record achievements, and submit scores. This data is provided and stored by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies: Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Apple ID, Google Account email, real name, or password.

Cloud save (iCloud / Google Play Games cloud save)

The Game can save your progress and settings (for example level, score, currency, and preferences) to your personal iCloud account (iOS) or your Google Play Games cloud save / Android device backup (Android), so you can keep playing across devices and restore progress after reinstalling. This data is stored in your own Apple or Google account storage, is managed by Apple or Google, and is not accessible to us. You can turn this off in Settings > [your name] > iCloud on iOS, or in the Google Play Games app settings / Settings > Google > Backup on Android.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store or by Google through Google Play Billing, depending on your device. We do not see or store your payment card details, billing address, Apple ID, or Google Account credentials. We only receive confirmation from Apple or Google that a purchase was completed so we can unlock the purchased item, and we may store that purchase status locally on your device or in your cloud save so you can restore it.

Advertising

The Game shows advertisements provided by third-party ad networks. To deliver, measure, and limit the frequency of ads, these partners may collect or receive:

  • Device advertising identifiers, such as the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA, iOS) if you grant permission, the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV, iOS), or the Android Advertising ID (AAID, Android);
  • IP address and approximate location (derived from IP address, not precise GPS);
  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, language, app version);
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, viewed, or tapped) and other product interactions, such as video views;
  • Diagnostic and performance data (for example crash logs and app launch time), used to fix problems and to help measure and improve ads.
