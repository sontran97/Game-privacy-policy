2. Information we handle

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the Game may use your Game Center player alias and player ID to show leaderboards, record achievements, and submit scores. This data is provided and stored by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Apple ID, real name, or email address.

iCloud

The Game can save your progress and settings (for example level, score, currency, and preferences) to your personal iCloud account so you can keep playing across your devices and restore progress after reinstalling. This data is stored in your own iCloud storage, is managed by Apple, and is not accessible to us. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment card details, billing address, or Apple ID. We only receive confirmation from Apple that a purchase was completed so we can unlock the purchased item, and we may store that purchase status locally on your device or in iCloud so you can restore it.

Advertising

The Game shows advertisements provided by third-party ad networks. To deliver, measure, and limit the frequency of ads, these partners may collect or receive:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) if you grant permission, or the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV);
  • IP address and approximate location (derived from IP address, not precise GPS);
  • Device and app information (device model, iOS version, language, app version);
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, viewed, or tapped) and other product interactions, such as video views;
  • Diagnostic and performance data (for example crash logs and app launch time), which is used to fix problems and to help measure and improve ads.
